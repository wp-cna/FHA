Join the FHA

Become a member.

Membership keeps the Association running and gives you a voice in what happens around the neighborhood. Dues are modest and support events and advocacy on behalf of Fisher Hill.

Members receive neighborhood updates, a say in Association positions before the city and the WPCNA, and an invitation to every meeting and gathering.

Who can join

Membership is open to current Fisher Hill residents and to former residents who'd like to stay connected. If you've moved away, just list the Fisher Hill address where you used to live.

Dues

Annual dues are $5 for an individual or $10 for a family. Submit the request below; after a board member verifies your Fisher Hill connection, the Association will email the current payment instructions.

Payment follow-up

Submit the membership request below. After a board member verifies your Fisher Hill connection, the Association will email the current payment options and instructions. No payment is collected on this website.
